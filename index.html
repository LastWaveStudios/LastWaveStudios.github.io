Rhythm Citadel

What if silence wasn't peace... but destruction? In this world, the notes protect the ballerina with their lives as she spins on her platform to keep the melody—and the balance of the box—alive.

Lead this group of instruments to save the music box. Only those with a sharp ear, a fine sense of rhythm, and a strategic mind will be able to save it.

Are you one of them?

A LITTLE ABOUT US

We are an indie studio made up of dreamers and passionate video game enthusiasts. Our mission is to create fresh, immersive gameplay experiences with a unique personality.

We are currently developing our first video game: a rhythmic tower defense set inside a steampunk-style music box where you protect the ballerina from mechanical notes.

This is just the beginning of our journey, and we're excited to share every step with you. Follow us on social media for updates and behind-the-scenes content.
